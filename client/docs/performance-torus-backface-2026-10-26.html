Aether fixed and rotating views gain about 6–7% in this run. Moving Aether improves its frame p95 from 22.3 to 18.7 ms. Copper's slow-frame tail stays at about 25 ms despite a modest average improvement. Nature's tiny changes do not establish a performance benefit.

Recommendation

Keep the original default. Aether is an optional aggressive tradeoff only if the missing background islands are acceptable. Copper is the better visual candidate for further testing; its moving gain is small. Nature introduces thin sky-colored holes without a measured FPS gain. Long camera travel, cutoff popping and publication transitions need more evaluation before a persistent setting.

Controls, limitations and raw evidence

Same-machine offline repeated-district fixtures; one location per world. No GPU clock or thermal lock. Original-only Aether controls range 107.05–115.07 FPS before and 108.55–111.53 after, demonstrating background variability. All fixed/rotating Aether adjacent pairs improve, but one moving pair does not. Copper moving pairs improve by 0.5–7.0%. GPU controls drift materially, so span percentages are observations rather than portable guarantees.

Near meshes and unpacked transition fallbacks are unchanged. Resident geometry is retained, so this does not save geometry memory. CPU proxy setup is outside timing; per-position culling work is inside. Nature and Copper each create one proxy during a moving block. Ordinary pending publication remains in the measurements. No uncaptured WebGPU errors were recorded.
